Advice From Sergey

Small rituals that protect the pleasure of a fine cigar.

Humidity

How to store cigars

Keep them away from heat, sunlight and dry air. Stable humidity is what preserves the wrapper, aroma and draw.

Flame

How to light a cigar properly

Toast the foot slowly before drawing. Let the heat prepare the tobacco instead of burning it aggressively.

Tempo

How to enjoy a cigar slowly

A Habanos is not rushed. Pause between draws and let the flavor open in stages.

Pairing

Best drink pairings

Aged rum, espresso, cognac and dark tea can complement Cuban tobacco without overwhelming it.

Mistakes

Common beginner mistakes

Over-cutting, overheating and smoking too quickly flatten the experience. Good cigars reward restraint.
